Our mission

We empower young people in Myanmar through education. We support those under 20 who could not finish high school with scholarships that open the way to higher education and lifelong learning.

About us

Thingaha is a donor group based in Tokyo, Japan, working with local leaders and education teams in Myanmar.

In Ayeyarwaddy Division, the president of the monastic schools manages donations, accounting and day-to-day operations on the ground. For our GED scholarships, the Learning with Dr. Myo Thida team manages the funds, selects students, and handles accounting and logistics.

  • Khine Zar Thwe

    Founder and Organizer

    The contact point for current and potential donors. Reach out at hello@thingaha.org or message the Thingaha Facebook page.

  • Ashin Yaewatta

    Co-Organizer · President of the Monastic Schools Administration Council, Nyaung Done Township

    Manages staffing, finances and administration of 5 monastic schools in Nyaung Done. He finds children whose families cannot afford basic education, enrols them, connects them with Thingaha donors and handles payments in Myanmar.

  • Thet Paing Soe

    Cash Flow In Charge · Nyaung Done, Myanmar

    Manages the cash flow of donations, handling each donor’s contribution to Myanmar.

  • Hnin Oo Wai Hlaing

  • Thandar Khine Aye

  • Sis Minn Maw
